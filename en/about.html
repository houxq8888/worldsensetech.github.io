Hello, Welcome

I'm a robotics AI engineer with a Master's degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University and over a decade of experience in automation and AI. I previously authored two technical books: Visual C++ Made Simple and 300 Classic C++ Programming Examples. Currently, I focus on research and practice in World Models and Sim-to-Real Transfer.

This blog is my technical notebook, documenting my learning, thinking, and hands-on experience in this field. I try to explain complex technical concepts clearly and share practical engineering lessons learned along the way.

Why This Blog

Embodied AI is becoming the next frontier in AI. From ChatGPT to Sora, large language models and generative models have shown remarkable capabilities. But the next frontier is enabling AI to not just generate content in the digital world, but to understand and act in the physical world.

World models are a key technology for achieving this goal. They allow robots to predict the consequences of actions in "imagination," reducing reliance on real-world trial and error. This direction has great potential, but Chinese-language technical resources are relatively scarce. I hope to fill this gap through my writing.

Technical Focus Areas

  • World Models: DreamerV3, RSSM, imagination-space training
  • Sim-to-Real Transfer: Domain randomization, system identification, adaptive policies
  • Reinforcement Learning: Actor-Critic methods, offline policy learning
  • Robot Control: Motion planning, force control, task adaptation
  • Simulation Tools: MuJoCo, Isaac Sim, PyBullet
